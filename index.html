02Core scope: MEP maintenance

Four maintenance pillars, one accountable team

Every discipline is led by a specialised engineer, executed by licensed technicians, and closed out with a technical report.

01

Electrical & power systems

Safe, code-compliant power from the main panel down to the last light fitting.

  • Main and sub distribution panels, breakers and thermal imaging
  • Diesel generators: servicing, load testing, ATS panels
  • UPS units and battery banks
  • Wiring, earthing and short-circuit fault finding
  • Interior, façade and emergency lighting

02

HVAC & mechanical

Cooling that holds temperature through a Cairo August — and stays efficient.

  • Chillers: compressors, condensers, water treatment
  • Split, package and central AC units
  • Ventilation, ducting, fans and air balancing
  • Water and circulation pumps, valves and pipework
  • Filter, coil and refrigerant-charge programmes

03

Plumbing & firefighting

Water networks and life-safety systems maintained to civil-defence requirements.

  • Feed and drainage networks, tanks and leak detection
  • Fire pumps, jockey pumps and pressure testing
  • Sprinklers, hose reels and extinguisher refills
  • Fire alarm panels, detectors and sounders
  • Inspection reports for civil defence approvals

04

Industrial & facility maintenance

Preventive programmes that protect production hours and asset life.

  • Production lines, motors, gearboxes and conveyors
  • Elevators and escalators (with licensed partners)
  • Annual facility management contracts
  • Planned preventive maintenance schedules and asset registers
  • On-site resident technician options

03MEP engineering services

Beyond maintenance: the engineering behind it

Maintenance is our core scope, but the same engineering team also delivers the design and documentation work that MEP projects need — from shop drawings to commissioning.

Shop drawings (SD)

Coordinated MEP shop drawings and as-built drawings, ready for consultant approval.

MEP design

Electrical, HVAC, plumbing and firefighting design for new fit-outs, expansions and retrofits.

Load & sizing calculations

Cooling and electrical load studies, pump and duct sizing, panel schedules and cable sizing.

BOQ & tender documents

Quantity take-offs, specifications and tender packages so you can compare bids fairly.

Site supervision

Engineering supervision of contractors, snag lists and progress reports through to handover.

Testing & commissioning

TAB, functional testing, commissioning records and O&M documentation for handover.
